About Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetic retinopathy is a diabetes complication that affects the eyes. It's caused by damage to the blood vessels of the light-sensitive tissue at the back of the eye (retina).

At first, diabetic retinopathy may cause no symptoms or only mild vision problems. Eventually, it can cause blindness.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide accessible and accurate early detection of diabetic retinopathy using cutting-edge AI technology. We aim to prevent vision loss and improve the quality of life for people with diabetes.
